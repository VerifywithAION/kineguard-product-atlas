# Browser Protection

> **Public evidence class: TESTNET VERIFIED — HISTORICAL BOUNDED.** A visible control or illustrative UI cannot certify an operating protection mechanism.

## Mechanism and customer meaning

The MV3 adapter normalizes supported requests and can hold the exact provider handoff. KINE evaluates policy and communicates ALLOW/WARN/DENY; human Continue or Cancel is bound to the same request.

## Evidence actually available

Historical GP1A T1–T4 Chrome/MetaMask/Sepolia cases; read detailed results in evidence-and-proofs.

## Limits and release gate

This is not universal RPC interception. The merged customer-policy binding, provider-forward receipts and current deployment must be accepted separately.

## Forensic acceptance questions

- Is the action scoped to the **same account, wallet, chain, provider and policy revision**?
- Does the visible result distinguish observation, policy change, blocked provider handoff and chain execution?
- Does an expired request or a mismatched owner fail closed?
- Is the result supported by a reproducible runtime proof, rather than an interface label?
