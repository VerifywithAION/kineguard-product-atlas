# Recipient Rules

> **Public evidence class: LAB / CI VERIFIED — COVERED BROWSER.** A visible control or illustrative UI cannot certify an operating protection mechanism.

## Mechanism and customer meaning

A destination allow/deny rule only matters when the policy is bound to the wallet, chain, exact action, address and governing executor. UI editing and policy enforcement are independent gates.

## Evidence actually available

R1E integrated synthetic CI confirms account-owned saved recipient deny rules, cross-account read/write rejection and zero forwards for denied covered native Sepolia transfers. Historical governed rejection is separate; hosted customer acceptance remains pending.

## Limits and release gate

Do not claim a live editable recipient policy across external EOAs, all chains or every asset.

## Forensic acceptance questions

- Is the action scoped to the **same account, wallet, chain, provider and policy revision**?
- Does the visible result distinguish observation, policy change, blocked provider handoff and chain execution?
- Does an expired request or a mismatched owner fail closed?
- Is the result supported by a reproducible runtime proof, rather than an interface label?
