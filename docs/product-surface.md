# Product surface — customer view, not a second runtime

KINEGuard presents a consumer journey around an account, its wallet records, policy state, economic exposure and evidence. The product is not just a wallet extension or a set of nine controls.

## Account-first navigation

1. **Account** — authenticated customer session and owned records.
2. **Portfolio** — wallets, agents and tokenized assets; each object's authority is independent unless there is explicit canonical linkage.
3. **Wallet View Details** — the agreed order is: Position → Protection → Economic Exposure → Authority & Permissions → Hybrid Governance → Browser Protection → Activity → Evidence.
4. **Guardian** — supervision of posture, alerts, agent actions, exposure, pending weakening and decisions. Guardian is a customer-facing monitoring experience, **not** a synonym for the independent on-chain Safety Authority.
5. **Agent Arena** — identity, permissions, budget and actions; not automatic wallet-spend power.
6. **Activity / Receipts** — forensic lineage distinguishes observations, policy changes, provider handoff and chain confirmation.
7. **Admin** — internal beta administration and product operations, not a customer security authority.
8. **KINE AI** — future grounded explanation, investigation and proposal; no signing authority.

## Recording/demo datasets vs. customer truth

The previously accepted isolated presentation used synthetic wallet, agent and asset records for demonstration and filming. These cannot be mislabeled as the user's real on-chain portfolio, treasury, customer acquisition, TVL or live protection. Local screenshots included in this repository show the **Atlas**, not a populated customer session.
