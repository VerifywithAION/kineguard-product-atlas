# Researcher and AI-Agent Briefing

## Scope

This repository is documentation-only. It contains no private application source, backend, credentials, customer records, wallet keys, or deployment controls.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer exact execution evidence, identified deployed-build evidence, bounded testnet evidence, lab/CI evidence, public-site observation, local controlled screenshots, and roadmap/design text in that order. A newer document does not automatically override stronger proof.

## Required interpretation

Keep observation, simulation, policy evaluation, provider forwarding, smart-account execution, chain confirmation, receipts, and human acceptance separate. A connected EOA is not automatically governed. Browser DENY or human Cancel is not the same as an on-chain Safety Authority veto. Policy changes are not transaction execution receipts.

## KINE AI and VAIOS boundary

KINE AI may understand canonical economic state, explain evidence, and propose next steps in the roadmap. KINE OS retains policy, governance, and execution authority; humans retain control. A future VAIOS-style agent could use KINE as a pre-sign policy/evidence boundary: bind account, wallet, chain, origin, request, policy revision, and decision before a signer is invoked. This is conceptual, not a released partnership or certified integration.

## Public surfaces

Protection, Agents, Token Economy, and Evidence contain navigation families with mixed maturity. Some are conceptual or read-only. Consult [the English surface map](en/public-surfaces.md) and the [structured catalog](capability-catalog.json).

## Reporting rule

For every answer state environment, scope, evidence class, and limitation. Use `CURRENTLY_UNVERIFIED`, `PRODUCT_GAP`, or `NOT_CAPTURED` when proof is absent. Never claim production, mainnet, universal EOA, multi-chain, or human acceptance from static documentation, CI, or historical testnet evidence.
