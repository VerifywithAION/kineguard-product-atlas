## 6. Financial model required before fundraising

Replace envelopes with a dated monthly model of contractor compensation and taxes, cloud/RPC costs, independent audits, incident reserves, legal costs, pilot acquisition, price, usage, gross margin, conversion, churn, runway and scenario sensitivity. Use conservative/base/upside assumptions with verifiable vendor bids and actual revenue evidence. Do not invent TAM/SAM/SOM or a valuation.

## 7. Proprietary technology and partner interfaces

Publicly describe the problem, interface contracts, allowed inputs/outputs, synthetic tests and proven results. Keep private the sensitive policy engine, internal Economic Exposure logic, Hybrid Governance implementation details, private security findings, secrets and customer datasets. A neutral adapter may expose ALLOW/WAIT/DENY plus decision/evidence identifiers without transferring the implementation to a payment platform. Local evaluation does not guarantee resistance to reverse engineering; contractual and legal controls matter.

## 8. Operating risks and hiring gates

A missing receipt, signer-bypass route, race in shared reservations or stale owner authorization is a launch blocker where that guarantee is advertised. A green CI badge alone is not human beta acceptance. Require independent QA, explicit negative controls, owner-approved deployment and release-specific proofs. Hire chain specialists only as each adapter earns real demand, not because a roadmap lists many ecosystems.

## 9. Public versus controlled diligence

**Public:** roles sought, technical evidence, planned milestones, broad indicative capital scenarios and selected interfaces. **Controlled data room:** actual salaries/bids, bank/cap table, investor terms, contracts, internal code, security exposures and permitted customer information. A secret GitHub gist is not a protected confidential data room.
