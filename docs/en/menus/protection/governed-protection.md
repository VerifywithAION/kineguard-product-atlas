## 1. Purpose

Explains the boundary between inspection UI, extension, SDK Alpha and governed smart accounts.

## 2. How the user operates it

1. Open Governed Protection and read the four layers and their independent status.
2. Check exactly which asset, method, chain and path are governed; a connected EOA remains an EOA.
3. Consult historical V3 3B proof: allowed action, pending, Safety Authority veto and matured execution.
4. Compare those tests with public beta availability and receipts before claiming present enforcement.

## 3. Underlying mechanism

This route belongs to the Protection family and must be interpreted with identity, policy, exact request and evidence provenance.

## 4. Evidence and limitations

Historical native ETH Sepolia V3 tests do not certify ERC-20, every chain or mainnet.

A visible public screen does not equal human runtime acceptance or universal funds protection.
