# Protection / Approval Intelligence

[ES](../../../../docs/es/menus/protection/approval-intelligence.md) · [EN](../../../../docs/en/menus/protection/approval-intelligence.md) · [Index](../../index.md)

**Route:** [/approvals](https://kineguard.com/approvals) · **Kind:** standalone page

## 1. Purpose

Inspects allowances, spenders, tokens and related risk in read-only mode.

## 2. How the user operates it

1. Open Approval Intelligence; choose demo mode or a compatible connected wallet for a read-only scan.
2. If Approval evidence not available appears, do not interpret zero as proof of no approvals.
3. Review token, spender, allowance, unlimited-permission alerts and contract risk when evidence exists.
4. Open Advanced approval intelligence for history and JSON/CSV export when available. Continue to Action Review or Guardian.

## 3. Underlying mechanism

This route belongs to the Protection family and must be interpreted with identity, policy, exact request and evidence provenance.

## 4. Evidence and limitations

This page does not auto-revoke, sign, broadcast transactions or move funds.

A visible public screen does not equal human runtime acceptance or universal funds protection.
