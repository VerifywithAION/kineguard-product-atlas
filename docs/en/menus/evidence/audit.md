## 1. Purpose

Availability state of the hosted audit archive.

## 2. How the user operates it

1. Open Evidence > Audit and read the availability notice.
2. The page states that a hosted immutable archive is not available in this beta.
3. Use History, Structured Records and Sessions for records actually available.
4. Do not present Audit as certified enterprise retention or legal proof.

## 3. Underlying mechanism

This route belongs to the Evidence family and must be interpreted with identity, policy, exact request and evidence provenance.

## 4. Evidence and limitations

There is no operational hosted immutable audit archive in this beta.

A visible public screen does not equal human runtime acceptance or universal funds protection.
