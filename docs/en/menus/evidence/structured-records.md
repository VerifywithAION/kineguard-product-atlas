## 1. Purpose

Normalized decisions and their provenance trail.

## 2. How the user operates it

1. Open Structured Records within /receipts.
2. Identify decision ID, receipt ID, event ID, policy revision and source if present.
3. Verify provider or chain outcome separately before attributing execution.
4. Follow exact testnet or CI evidence references; do not treat placeholders as final receipts.

## 3. Underlying mechanism

This route belongs to the Evidence family and must be interpreted with identity, policy, exact request and evidence provenance.

## 4. Evidence and limitations

A POLICY_CHANGE receipt is not a blockchain receipt or on-chain finality.

A visible public screen does not equal human runtime acceptance or universal funds protection.
