# Cryptographic agility and future transitions

The KINE research lineage distinguishes **cryptographic authentication** from **economic execution permission**. Future changes of algorithm, credential type or signature scheme should preserve owner/authority provenance, policy revision, exact request identity, economic limits and receipt lineage.

This is a **research and design doctrine**. Current KINEGuard is **not certified quantum-safe or post-quantum-ready**. Quantum or Qiskit/Aer analytical scenarios in the extended AION roadmap are advisory research, not additional veto/signer powers. Any new implementation would require explicit compatibility, migration safety and test evidence for the named chain and wallet type.
