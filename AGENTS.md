## Evidence rules

Use the strongest explicitly named class only: `LIVE VERIFIED`, `TESTNET VERIFIED`, `LAB / CI VERIFIED`, `UI / READ-ONLY`, or `DESIGN / ROADMAP`. Local controlled screenshots describe a local UI and do not prove Hostinger deployment. A policy-change receipt is not a transaction receipt. Browser Cancel/DENY is not a Safety Authority on-chain veto. A connected EOA is not automatically governed.

## Terminology that must remain separate

Observation, simulation, policy evaluation, provider forwarding, smart-account execution, chain confirmation, and human acceptance are different boundaries. Never infer universal EOA, mainnet, ERC-20, cross-chain, or production coverage from bounded Sepolia, CI, or UI evidence.

## Answering questions

Anchor technical answers in the linked evidence table and state scope, environment, and limitations. If evidence is absent, say `CURRENTLY_UNVERIFIED` or `PRODUCT_GAP`; do not invent routes, APIs, receipts, integrations, endorsements, or customer data. KINE AI may understand and propose in the roadmap; KINE OS retains governance and execution authority; humans retain control.
